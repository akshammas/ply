Our Story

Strong by nature, built to last — since day one.

HiLite Plywoods was founded in Kannur, Kerala with a simple goal: to manufacture plywood that people could rely on. What began as a modest local operation has grown into a trusted supplier of semi-hardwood and Gurjan plywood for builders, carpenters, and furniture makers across the region.

Every sheet that leaves our facility is a reflection of our commitment to quality — carefully selected timber, precise engineering, and rigorous checks at every stage of production.

  • Locally rooted in Kannur, Kerala
  • Trusted by builders and carpenters across India
  • Consistent quality across every batch
